# Marketing Assets Plan

## Website Feature List
1. Smart Invoicing — Multi-template, custom fields, auto-numbering
2. Multi-Client Management — Customer database with history
3. Financial Reports — Revenue tracking, due ledger, expenses
4. PDF Export — Print-ready, shareable PDFs
5. Live Invoice Links — Public shareable pages with payment
6. UPI / bKash / Nagad Payment — Integrated payment proof system
7. Inventory Management — Stock tracking for products/services
8. Multi-Template Support — Classic, Modern, Corporate, etc.
9. Dark Mode / Themes — Customizable premium themes
10. Multi-Workspace — Manage multiple businesses
11. Android App — Capacitor mobile experience
12. Offline Support — Local-first with cloud sync
13. Data Backup & Restore — Export/import business data
14. Verification Codes — Secure invoice verification
